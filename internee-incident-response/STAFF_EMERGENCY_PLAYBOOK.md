# Internee.pk Staff Emergency Response Playbook (Ransomware & Breaches)

## 1. Disconnect & Report — All Internee.pk Staff
- Disconnect affected workstation from network/Wi-Fi and alert Security Lead immediately; never pay ransom.

## 2. Contain & Preserve — SOC / Incident Lead
- Isolate host, revoke active sessions/tokens, capture volatile logs/IOCs, and block malicious domains/IPs.

## 3. Recover & Verify — Cloud & Infra Team
- Validate offline/immutable backups, restore clean snapshots, verify SHA-256 hashes, and monitor for re-entry.

## 4. Post-Incident Review — Leadership & Comms
- Conduct root-cause analysis, complete breach/reporting obligations if required, and update detection rules.
